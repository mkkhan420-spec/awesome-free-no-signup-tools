# Awesome Free Tools (No Signup)

A curated list of free web tools that work with no signup and no login.

Entries marked ⭐ are our own tools (Alternative Kits). The rest are independent tools we recommend.

## Text & Writing

- [Fancy Text Generator ⭐](https://alternativekits.com/tools/fancy-text-generator/) - Converts plain text into 32 Unicode styles for Instagram bios, posts and usernames, with a copy button per style and copy-all; free, no signup and no ads.
- [Italic Text Generator ⭐](https://alternativekits.com/tools/italic-text-generator/) - Converts plain text into italic Unicode styles to copy and paste into bios, posts and comments, free with no signup.
- [LingoJam Fancy Text Generator](https://lingojam.com/FancyTextGenerator) - Converts normal text into fancy text with an online translator to copy and paste.
- [YayText](https://yaytext.com/) - Generates Unicode text styles to copy and paste into Facebook, Twitter and other sites.
- [Diff Checker](https://www.diffchecker.com/) - Compares text, files and code to find differences online for free.
- [CopyPasteCharacter](https://www.copypastecharacter.com/) - Lets you copy the hidden characters from computer typefaces.
- [LanguageTool](https://languagetool.org/) - Checks grammar, punctuation and spelling in over 30 languages.
- [DeepL Translate](https://www.deepl.com/translator) - Translates texts and full document files instantly.

## Images

- [TinyPNG](https://tinypng.com/) - Compresses AVIF, JPEG XL, WebP, PNG and JPEG images to reduce file size for free.
- [Remove.bg](https://www.remove.bg/) - Removes image backgrounds automatically in a few seconds with one click.
- [Photopea](https://www.photopea.com/) - Edits photos in the browser with effects, filters, text, cropping and resizing for free.
- [Squoosh](https://squoosh.app/) - Compresses and compares images with different codecs in the browser.
- [iLoveIMG](https://www.iloveimg.com/) - Modifies images in seconds for free with cropping, resizing, compression and conversion.

## Converters

- [Convertio](https://convertio.co/) - Converts files online across more than 309 document, image, spreadsheet, ebook, archive, presentation, audio and video formats.
- [CloudConvert](https://cloudconvert.com/) - Converts files across more than 200 audio, video, document, ebook, archive, image, spreadsheet and presentation formats.
- [Smallpdf](https://smallpdf.com/) - Converts and edits PDF files online for free.
- [iLovePDF](https://www.ilovepdf.com/) - Works with PDF files online for free with merging, splitting, compression, office to PDF and PDF to JPG.
- [Online-Convert](https://www.online-convert.com/) - Converts images, video, documents, audio and more to other formats for free.
- [Zamzar](https://www.zamzar.com/) - Converts video, audio, images and ebooks online for free with no download or account required.

## Productivity

- [Invoice Maker ⭐](https://alternativekits.com/tools/invoice-maker/) - Creates invoices in the browser with 30 currencies and a one-page print, free with no signup.
- [Pomofocus](https://pomofocus.io/) - Runs a Pomodoro timer in the desktop or mobile browser to manage time and focus on tasks.
- [World Time Buddy](https://www.worldtimebuddy.com/) - Converts time across zones and schedules conference calls, webinars and meetings.
- [Time.is](https://time.is/) - Shows the exact time in 7 million locations, synchronized with atomic clock time.
- [Calculator.net](https://www.calculator.net/) - Offers free online calculators for math, finance, fitness and science.
- [Online Stopwatch](https://www.online-stopwatch.com/) - Runs a free online stopwatch and countdown in the browser.
- [Every Time Zone](https://everytimezone.com/) - Compares time zones and finds meeting times with a visual converter.

## Dev Tools

- [JSON Formatter](https://jsonformatter.org/) - Formats and validates JSON data, and converts JSON to XML and CSV.
- [Regex101](https://regex101.com/) - Builds, tests and debugs regular expressions with live matches and explanations.
- [JSFiddle](https://jsfiddle.net/) - Tests React, JavaScript, CSS and HTML online in a code playground.
- [Can I Use](https://caniuse.com/) - Shows up-to-date browser support tables for front-end web technologies.
- [DevDocs](https://devdocs.io/) - Searches documentation for over 100 programming languages and tools in one web app.
- [Carbon](https://carbon.now.sh/) - Creates and shares images of source code.

## Privacy & Security

- [Have I Been Pwned](https://haveibeenpwned.com/) - Checks if an email address has been exposed in a data breach.
- [DuckDuckGo](https://duckduckgo.com/) - Searches and browses the internet without tracking or storing search history.
- [Privacy Badger](https://privacybadger.org/) - Blocks hidden trackers while you browse the web, built by the EFF.
- [Guerrilla Mail](https://www.guerrillamail.com/) - Gives out disposable temporary email addresses.
- [Startpage](https://www.startpage.com/) - Searches the internet without tracking or storing search history.
- [EmailOnDeck](https://www.emailondeck.com/) - Provides free temporary and disposable email addresses.

## Utilities

- [Barcode Generator ⭐](https://alternativekits.com/tools/barcode-generator/) - Creates EAN-13, CODE128, EAN-8, UPC-A, CODE39 and ITF-14 barcodes in the browser with PNG download, free with no signup.
- [Hashtag Generator ⭐](https://alternativekits.com/tools/hashtag-generator/) - Generates copy-paste hashtag sets for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X and Facebook from curated lists, free with no signup.
- [QR Code Generator](https://www.qr-code-generator.com/) - Creates free QR codes for URLs, vCards and more with logos, colors and frames.
- [Speedtest](https://www.speedtest.net/) - Tests broadband speed on desktop and mobile devices for free.
- [Is It Down Right Now](https://www.isitdownrightnow.com/) - Checks whether a website is down for everyone or just for you.
- [TinyURL](https://tinyurl.com/) - Shortens long URLs into short links, with branded links and analytics.
- [Wayback Machine](https://web.archive.org/) - Archives web pages and lets you browse old versions of them for free.
- [StagePeek](https://stagepeek.live/) - Previews 2D seating charts and 3D concert stage sightlines across 201 global venues with 1x-10x zoom and no login required.
